# Convex Hull

A Convex hull is the union of all half-spaces that lean on my point cloud. In other words it's the minimal convex solid that I can do around an object.
$$CH(S) = R^d \setminus \bigcup EH(S) $$
Where $EH(S)$ is the halfspace not containing any point is S.
