# Wordclouds

Representation of how frequently words appear in a given body of text through the size of the word. Variations in arrangement and color. Mainly used for aesthetic reasons

![[Pasted image 20251215202146.png | 500]]
### Calligrams
- Texts arranged so that they form a thematically related image
- The image created by the words illustrates the text by expressing visually something associated to (or even contradicting) what the text says
