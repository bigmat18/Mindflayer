# Vector Visualization
Attributes are represented by vectors:
- e.g., rate of changes, deformations due to forces, etc.
- vectors have magnitude and direction
With problems involving derivatives of scalar fields. 

The easy way: **draw arrows**. No interpolation required, overall impression of the field, but **problems with occlusions**
- Magnitude mapped to length, width, colour
- 3D: also perspective distortion (interaction needed to recover depth cues)
