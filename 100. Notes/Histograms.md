# Histograms

Histograms visualize the distribution of values of a quantitative attribute. Same marks and channels as bar charts, but:
- we are showing derivated (aggregated) data
- usually there is no space between bars
- being a contonuous distribution, it makes no sense to oder bars
- the bin number and size can be changed to get a clearer picture of the distribution
