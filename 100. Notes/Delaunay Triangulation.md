# Delaunay Triangulation

In **computational geometry**, a **Delaunay Triangulation** of a set of points is the plane subdivided their **[[Alpha Shapes|convex hull]]** into triangles whose circumcircles do not contain any of point.

Each circumcirlce has its generating points on its circumference, but alla other points in the set are outside of it.

This maximizes the size of the smallest angle in any of the triangles, and tends to avoid [sliver triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sliver_triangle "Sliver triangle").
