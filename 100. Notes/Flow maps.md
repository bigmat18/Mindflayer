# Flow maps

Depict the movement of entities in space and time by placing stroked lines on top of maps (space and time). Flow maps encode a large amount of multivariate information:
- thickness, color, etc. can encode additional information

![[Pasted image 20251215201739.png]]

In this map: size of troops, distance travelled, temperature, latitude and longitude, direction of travel, location relative to time
