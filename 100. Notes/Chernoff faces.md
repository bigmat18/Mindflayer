# Chernoff faces

Rationale: we are very good at recognizing faces. Introduced by Herman Chernoff in 1973
- Variables are mapped to facial features (width/curvature of mouth, vertical size of face, size/slant/separation of eyes, size of eyebrows, vertical position of eyebrows...)

![[Pasted image 20251215202531.png | 500]]

Variables are mapped on facial features:
- Width/curvature of mouth
- Vertical size of the face
- Size/slant/separation of eyes
- Size of eyebrows
- Vertical position of eyebrows

Chernoff faces received some criticism:
- Difficult to compare.
- A legend is necessary.

Moreover, the mapping should be choose carefully to work properly.
