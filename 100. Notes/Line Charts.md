# Line Charts

A line chart visualizes:
- an ordered attribute (key) using horizontal position as a channel (often time, but not mandatory)
- a quantitative attribute (value) using vertical position as a channel

The marks are points and line segments connecting consecutive point pairs
- segments are connection marks to emphasize the ordering of the items along the key axis by explicitly showing the relationship between one item and the next
- thus, they have a stronger implication of trend relationships, making them suitable for the task of spotting trends
- likely applications: finance, climate studies, medicine and public policies...
