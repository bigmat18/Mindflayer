# Popout

Many visual channels provide visual popout, where a distinct item stands out from many others immediately
- added value of popout: if the time it takes us to spot a different object does not depend on the number of distractor objects

![[Pasted image 20251215191618.png | 500]]

![[Pasted image 20251215191635.png | 350]]

As a general rule, use popout for a single channel at a time:
- most pair of channels do not support popout (but shape and color do)
- popout is not possible with three or more channels
