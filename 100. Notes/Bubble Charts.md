# Bubble Charts

Variant on [[Scatterplots]], accommodated to represent additional attributes. Circle areas and colors can
represent additional attributes (four in this example)

![[Pasted image 20251215195931.png | 500]]

One could even add more channels and attributes, but the more attributes you add, the harder it is to decode the information that you are trying to pass on (the rule is never exhaggerate)

Too many bubbles can make the chart hard to read. Therefore, bubble charts have a limited data size capacity (scalability). Again, this can be coped with through [[Basics of Data Visualization#Why use interactivity?|interactivity]] 
- clicking or hovering over bubbles to display hidden information
- having an option to filter out categories
